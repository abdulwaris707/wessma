Meta Advantage+ and Google Performance Max changed paid media. The algorithms now find your audience better than any manual targeting setup. That leaves one lever with outsized impact: **creative**.

## Why creative fatigue kills performance

The same ad shown to the same audience loses effectiveness quickly. CPMs rise, click-through rates fall and cost per acquisition creeps up. Most brands respond by tweaking budgets. The real fix is fresh creative.

## Our weekly testing rhythm

Every week, for every account, our editing and ads teams run the same loop:

1. **Review** — which hooks, formats and angles won last week?
2. **Brief** — three to five new concepts built on the winning insights
3. **Produce** — our in-house editors cut the concepts within 48 hours
4. **Launch** — new concepts go into a structured testing campaign
5. **Scale** — winners graduate to the main campaign with more budget

## What we test

- **Hooks** — the first two seconds decide everything
- **Formats** — UGC-style, product demos, testimonials, carousels
- **Angles** — problem-first, outcome-first, social proof, offer-led
- **Length** — 6, 15 and 30 seconds

## Tracking makes it work

Creative testing only works when the algorithm gets clean conversion signals. We set up server-side tracking and the Conversions API before scaling any budget.
