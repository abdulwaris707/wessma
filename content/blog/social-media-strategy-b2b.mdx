Most B2B companies treat social media like a billboard: product announcements, event photos and the occasional hiring post. Then they wonder why it does not generate leads. **Buyers do not follow brands — they follow people and ideas.**

## Lead with people, not logos

Founder and expert profiles consistently outperform company pages on LinkedIn. We build content systems around two or three people in your company, with the company page amplifying their best work.

## Four content pillars

We plan every calendar around four pillars:

- **Point of view** — strong opinions about how your industry should work
- **Proof** — case studies, results and client stories
- **Process** — behind-the-scenes looks at how you work
- **Personal** — the human stories that build trust

## Repurpose everything

One podcast episode or webinar can become a long-form article, five short videos, three carousels and a dozen text posts. Our editing team turns one recording session into a month of content.

## Connect social to your CRM

Track which posts drive profile visits, website sessions and demo requests. Tag inbound leads with their first touchpoint. When social becomes measurable, it gets the budget it deserves.

## Consistency beats virality

Three good posts a week for six months will outperform one viral post every time. We plan monthly, publish daily and review results every week.
